# Kiro Dungeon Achievements

Funny, lightweight achievements for designers and developers using Kiro.

The tone should feel playful and game-like without getting in the way of work. Achievement names should be short enough to work in a toast, tooltip, or profile badge.

---

## Display Guidelines

Achievements should be lightweight and optional.

Recommended presentation:

- Small toast or badge
- Short title
- One-line message
- Optional character reaction
- Gold glow for standard achievements
- Purple glow for rare achievements
- No blocking modal
- No sound by default
- Achievement animation should finish in under 1 second

Do not trigger achievements so frequently that they become noise. They should feel like small surprises during meaningful moments.
